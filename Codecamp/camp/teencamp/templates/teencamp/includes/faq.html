What age group is CodeCamp designed for?

+
CodeCamp is designed for students between 10 and 18 years, with learning activities tailored to different experience levels.

Does my child need previous programming experience?

+
No. Beginners are welcome. Our curriculum starts with the fundamentals before progressing to more advanced concepts.

Are laptops provided?

+
Students are encouraged to bring their own laptops. If limited loaner devices are available, details will be communicated before the camp begins.

How is my registration confirmed?

+
After payment verification, your registration is approved, a batch is assigned automatically, and a confirmation message is sent.

Will students receive certificates?

+
Yes. Students who complete the program successfully will receive a Certificate of Participation at the end of the camp.

Can siblings be placed in the same batch?

+
Yes. Our registration system is designed to keep children from the same parent together whenever there is available capacity.
